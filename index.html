Good to know

Are these real Amazon deals? +

Not yet. The starter listings are clearly marked demos with illustrative product artwork and example prices. Real, verified listings can be added through products.js.

Will the website find Amazon deals automatically? +

Not in the static GitHub Pages version. That would require an approved data source and a backend integration. We have not added that yet.

Where are my saved products stored? +

Saved products stay in this browser's local storage on your device. There is no account or shared shopping cart.
