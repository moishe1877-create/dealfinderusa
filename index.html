GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity
goes a long way.

Everything you should know before exploring.

Are the products and prices on this site real? +

This starter website launches in preview mode. All preloaded products, illustrated artwork, discounts, and prices are sample design content, not current Amazon offers. The owner can replace them with genuine affiliate listings.

How do I add real Amazon affiliate products? +

Edit the easy-to-read products.js file: enter the real product title, description, category, image, and your Amazon Associates product link. Set status: "live". Published listings display a “View on Amazon” button without an unverified price.

Will clicking a product take me to Amazon? +

Real, published listings with a valid Amazon product link go to Amazon in a new tab. Sample listings open a preview window instead, so visitors are never sent to an invented deal.

How do saved deals work? +

Tap the heart on any product to save it. Your selections are stored on your own device, in this browser only. No account or sign-in is needed.

Does the website automatically find live Amazon deals? +

Not yet. This version is a fast, fully static GitHub Pages site. Adding automated Amazon data requires an authorized API or other permitted integration and a secure backend. Preview listings do not claim live prices or availability.
