Important about privacy

ⓘ
What the publisher does and doesn't do

Publishes real product information, not scraped Amazon prices or fake discounts. Full automatic publishing would require a secure backend or authorized GitHub integration. The website cannot safely keep passwords or API secrets in public JavaScript.
